Literature review

Bilingual grammarsWhile the two grammars of early bilingual speakers
develop largely independently, they also influence each other throughout
life in both childhood and adulthood.This interaction often results in
crosslinguistic transfer patterns, where speakers apply features or
structures from one language when using the other.From individual to
community:In cases of societal bilingualism (e.g., Catalan and Spanish
in Catalonia), individual-level transfer can accumulate over time,
driving language variation at the community level \(\rightarrow\)
variable input for early bilinguals.
\end{frame}
